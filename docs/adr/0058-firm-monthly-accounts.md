# ADR 0058: Concrete monthly firm accounts

- Status: Accepted foundation
- Date: 2026-07-23

Firm monthly accounts record actual sales revenue, wages owed, wages paid, and outstanding wage arrears from authoritative market and payroll settlements. The ledger is reset explicitly at a month boundary, saved, replayed, and fingerprinted. Employment decisions can now consume concrete cashflow evidence rather than a generic profitability score.
