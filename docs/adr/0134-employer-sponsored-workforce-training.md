# ADR 0134: Employer-sponsored workforce training

## Status
Accepted.

## Decision
If an eligible trainee cannot self-fund, a local firm facing the exact qualification shortage may pay tuition. Sponsorship requires a real vacancy, current cash, no arrears or insolvency, a forecast of at least three months, and cash coverage after inputs and payroll. Tuition is debited immediately. Sponsorship grants no forced employment or ownership of the worker.

## Consequences
Firms can invest in labor supply instead of only escalating wages, while training remains a risky real expenditure.
