# ADR 0031: Freight contracts and operating margins

- Status: Accepted foundation
- Date: 2026-07-22

Freight contracts identify shipper, carrier, route, reserved capacity, discount, term, and lifecycle. Route economics separately model fuel, labor, and maintenance cost per quantity unit. Shipment revenue applies active contract discounts; carrier margin may be negative, making bad contracts and cost shocks economically meaningful. Contract capacity enforcement and monthly cost settlement remain future World integration.
