# ADR 0052: Deprivation-derived social stress

- Status: Accepted foundation
- Date: 2026-07-22

Settled deprivation now produces two cohort signals. Health risk is the survival-tier unmet ratio; unrest pressure is weighted total deprivation. Signals are authoritative, replayable, saved, fingerprinted, and evented. They do not yet directly kill people or trigger protests; those causal transitions remain explicit later systems.
