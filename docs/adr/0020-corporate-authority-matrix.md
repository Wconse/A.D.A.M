# ADR 0020: Corporate authority matrix

- Status: Accepted foundation
- Date: 2026-07-21

Corporate authority is resolved per action, not through one universal control score. Majority voting owners retain broad authority. CEOs may set overall and functional policy and propose major investment. Operations and marketing managers receive narrow functional scopes. Board directors approve major investments, dividends, and executive appointments. Bylaws, voting coalitions, covenants, and law will compose with this matrix.
