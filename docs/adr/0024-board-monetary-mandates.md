# ADR 0024: Board monetary mandates

- Status: Accepted foundation
- Date: 2026-07-21

Approved one-time mandates may declare dividends or commit investment funds. Both require positive amounts and sufficient firm cash. Dividends require exactly 100% recorded economic ownership and distribute minor units deterministically by economic rights, assigning rounding remainder by canonical owner order. Investments move cash into an authoritative committed-investment balance. Execution emits events and cannot repeat.
