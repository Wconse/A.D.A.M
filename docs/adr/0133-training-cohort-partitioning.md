# ADR 0133: Training cohort partitioning

## Status
Accepted.

## Decision
When a multi-household cohort enters training, one representative household and its proportional people, income, wealth, and debt are split into a deterministic new cohort. All stocks remain exactly conserved and the source cohort continues its ordinary economic life. Single-household cohorts train intact.

## Consequences
Training no longer upgrades an arbitrarily large population at once. The same deterministic partition boundary can support later migration and household mobility.
