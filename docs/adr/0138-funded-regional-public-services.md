# ADR 0138: Funded regional public services

## Status
Accepted.

## Decision
Each region carries persistent healthcare, infrastructure, and administrative capacity. Thirty percent of recorded national fiscal spending is treated as the public-service envelope and reported as such rather than charged a second time. Funding relative to national output defines a bounded service target. Administrative capacity changes slowly and caps how quickly healthcare and infrastructure can absorb money. Existing service capacity affects demographic resilience and synthetic regional output growth in subsequent years.

## Consequences
High spending cannot instantly create functioning institutions, prolonged austerity erodes real capacity, and the same nominal budget can deliver different outcomes depending on administrative history. The explicit approximation can later be replaced by sector-specific facilities and staffing without changing the fiscal boundary.
