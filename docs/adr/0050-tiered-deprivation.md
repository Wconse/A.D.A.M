# ADR 0050: Tiered consumption and deprivation

- Status: Accepted foundation
- Date: 2026-07-22

Market orders, fills, consumption, and unmet demand preserve NeedTier. Shortages of survival goods are now distinguishable from participation, development, and discretionary shortfalls. Tiered ledgers are authoritative, replayable, saved, and fingerprinted for later health and political effects.
