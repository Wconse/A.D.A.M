# ADR 0051: Weighted deprivation pressure

- Status: Accepted foundation
- Date: 2026-07-22

Each settled month derives a 0–10,000 deprivation pressure per cohort from fulfilled plus unmet quantities. Survival shortages weigh 4, participation 3, development 2, and discretionary 1. The signal is authoritative and fingerprinted for later health, migration, protest, and reputation effects. Cross-good unit normalization remains a future refinement.
